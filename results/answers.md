### q07: Can a company whose property investment business is taxed under section 10D deduct revenue expenses it incurred before it earned any rental income?

Therefore, revenue expenses incurred before the first dollar of rental (or other investment) income are not deductible. The only possible deduction would be if the expenses relate to income‑producing investments, in which case they are deductible against that income alone.
